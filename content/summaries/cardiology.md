# Cardiology — High-Yield Summary

## Ischemic Heart Disease
- **Stable angina**: predictable exertional chest pain, relieved by rest/nitroglycerin. First-line: beta-blockers, nitrates, antiplatelet therapy, statin.
- **Acute coronary syndrome (ACS)**: unstable angina, NSTEMI, STEMI. STEMI requires emergent reperfusion (PCI preferred, within 90 minutes door-to-balloon).
- ECG localization: II/III/aVF = inferior (RCA), V1-V4 = anterior (LAD), I/aVL/V5-V6 = lateral (LCx).

## Valvular Disease
- **Aortic stenosis**: crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur, radiates to carotids, classic triad = angina, syncope, heart failure.
- **Mitral regurgitation**: holosystolic murmur at apex, radiates to axilla.
- **Aortic regurgitation**: early diastolic decrescendo murmur, widened pulse pressure.
- **Mitral stenosis**: mid-diastolic rumble with opening snap, most often rheumatic in origin.

## Heart Failure
- **HFrEF** (EF ≤ 40%): GDMT = ACEi/ARB/ARNI + beta-blocker + MRA + SGLT2 inhibitor, all mortality-reducing.
- **HFpEF** (EF ≥ 50%): manage volume status and comorbidities; SGLT2 inhibitors also show benefit.
- S3 gallop → volume overload; S4 gallop → stiff/hypertrophied ventricle.

## Pericardial Disease
- **Acute pericarditis**: pleuritic chest pain relieved by sitting forward, diffuse ST elevation with PR depression on ECG.
- **Cardiac tamponade**: Beck's triad (hypotension, JVD, muffled heart sounds), pulsus paradoxus, electrical alternans.

## Arrhythmias
- **Atrial fibrillation**: irregularly irregular rhythm, no discrete P waves. Manage with rate/rhythm control + stroke risk stratification (CHA₂DS₂-VASc).
- **WPW syndrome**: delta wave, short PR interval. Avoid AV nodal blockers if AFib is present — risk of degeneration to VFib.
