# Getting started

Medicine is a free study app for medical school. Everything in it is organised
the way the course is: by **study block** (1.1, 1.2, 1.3…) and then by
**subject** (anatomy, histology, physiology…). Each subject can have
flashcards, image-occlusion figures, a quiz, an ebook, a summary and the
lecture slides. Each block can have timed practice exams.

## You don't need an account

Open the app and start studying. As a guest, everything works: reviews,
quizzes, exams, reading progress and your streak are saved in this browser on
this device.

An account is optional. It adds:

- the same progress on every device you sign in on ([Accounts and
  sync](/docs/account)),
- the [Class Drive](/docs/modules-and-drive) with the class's slides and
  recordings,
- [Alfond](/docs/alfond) and AI explanations,
- the [leaderboard](/docs/leaderboard) and the class average on the exam plan.

## Find your way around

- **Home** shows your streak, cards due, what you were last doing ("continue"),
  the tip of the day and a card for every subject, block by block.
- **Subjects** gives each subject one page with everything it has and what's
  due next. It's the quickest way into a subject.
- The **sidebar** groups the rest: _Study_ (exam plan, flashcards, image
  occlusion, quizzes, exams, Virtual Lab), _Read_ (ebooks, summaries, modules,
  Class Drive, 3D anatomy, knowledge map) and _You_ (progress, leaderboard,
  account, this help). On a phone the same groups are in the tab bar at the bottom.
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac)
  anywhere to jump to any page or subject by typing its name.

## Put your block first

On the **Account** page, set **Current block**. Home then shows that block
first. It works for guests too, and you can change it any time.

## Install it and use it offline

The app can be installed like a phone or desktop app:

- **Android / Chrome / Edge:** open the browser menu and choose **Install app**
  (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone / iPad:** open the site in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to
  Home Screen**.

After your first visit the app opens without an internet connection.
Flashcards, quizzes, exams, ebooks, summaries and the lab all work offline. A
lecture PDF works offline once you've opened it once. The things that need a
connection are signing in, syncing, the Class Drive and Alfond.

## Updates

When a new version is out, a small **A new version is ready** bar appears.
Press **Refresh** when it suits you. The app never reloads on its own, so a
quiz or exam in progress is never lost.

The bottom of every page shows the block you're studying (your block from
**Account**, or the one with the next exam) and the app's version and build,
for example **v0.1.49 · build fb0fd76**. Mention the version when you report a
problem.

## Light or dark

The app follows your device's light or dark setting. Use the sun/moon button in
the sidebar to switch.
