# Knowledge map

The knowledge map shows every concept in the course as one picture: about 700
terms from the ebooks and summaries, across all blocks and subjects. Two
concepts are linked when they come up together, in the same paragraph,
flashcard or quiz question. A concept taught in two subjects is one dot that
joins them.

## Reading the map

- Each **dot** is a concept; bigger dots are mentioned more often.
- Each **line** is a link; the colour of a dot is its subject.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and use **Show the whole map**
  to reset the view.

## Tap a concept

The panel that opens shows:

- **Linked to:** the concepts it connects with. Tap one to move to it.
- **Read about it:** the exact ebook and summary sections about it.
- **Practise it:** its flashcards (opened as just those cards), its quiz
  questions (as a drill) and the labelled figures that show it.
- **Ask Alfond** how it connects to the rest (signed in).

**Focus on its links** hides everything else, so you can see one concept's
neighbourhood. **Show everything** brings the rest back.

## Find and filter

- **Find a concept…** searches the map by name.
- Narrow it to one **Block** or **Subject**.
- **Only links between subjects** hides links within one subject: good for
  seeing how anatomy, histology and physiology fit together. Before you pick a
  concept, the side panel lists the **concepts that connect subjects**.
- **List view** shows the same concepts as a list, which is easier on a small
  screen or with a screen reader.

## Colour by my mastery

**Color by my mastery** colours each concept by how well you know it, from the
flashcards, labels and quiz questions about it. The stronger the colour, the
better you know it. Concepts below halfway are ringed as **weak**, and
concepts you haven't studied stay grey. It's a quick way to find what to study
next.
